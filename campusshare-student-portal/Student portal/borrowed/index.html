Drop-off & Return Protocol

MANDATORY

Follow these 4 sequential steps when surrendering equipment to guarantee its tamper-resistant return and maintain zero-penalty status.

  1. 1
    Prep Accessories & Case

    Ensure all unit components are bundled securely (slide case and USB cable) before finding the designated return casing.

  2. 2
    Present Digital QR ID

    Scan your Student QR ID (2026) at the Science Complex check-in desk to authenticate custody.

  3. 3
    2-Minute Physical Inspection

    Complete brief physical diagnostic checks alongside the desk by a lab custodian to verify Grade-A condition.

  4. 4
    Digital Sign-off & Receipt

    Final digital sign-off confirms responsibility has ended; retain your automatically generated receipt.
